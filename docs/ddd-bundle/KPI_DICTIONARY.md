# KPI Dictionary

## KPI Catalog

### KPI: Customer Request Follow-up Readiness
- definition: Ability to enter the weekly customer update meeting with a complete, reproducible view of request workload and overdue follow-up.
- owner: Account Service Lead
- target: `null`; approval owner = Account Service Lead / Project Owner.
- frequency: weekly
- business_impact: reduces missed follow-up and makes ownership of overdue requests visible.
- OKR / iKPI mapping: `null`; owner = Project Owner.
